# Use the client area as the coordinate origin

Window-relative positions are signed offsets from the target window's client-area origin rather than its outer frame. This keeps application content anchored independently of non-client borders and shadows while still supporting title bars and owned transient surfaces through negative or out-of-bounds offsets. A window adjustment gesture freezes that origin at button press until release so the moving window cannot feed its new position back into the recorded drag path. Initial client-area size and DPI remain strict binding requirements, and DPI remains fixed after binding. Transitions to or from screen-relative movement are replayed directly without synthesized interpolation, because extra pointer paths could trigger unrecorded hover or drag behavior.
